## New Business — Planning and Zoning Resolutions (two-month rule; vote in October)

- Resolutions **2529**, **2530**, and **2531** are planning/zoning items subject to the **two-month** rule; **no vote tonight** — expected in the **October** county commission meeting.

### Resolution 2529 — R1 zoning in the growth area

- Applies to the **growth area** (described as roughly **60 square miles** near **Bon Aqua**), not **A1** agriculture district.
- **Intent:** R1 was created to ease subdivision development where developers bear road/water costs; in practice, R1 requests have often been on roads unsuitable for large subdivisions.
- Proposed standards before R1 rezoning: minimum **30-foot right-of-way** on the road and **potable water** on the road (Bon Aqua utility area); developer may improve road/water first, then rezone.
- **Feeder** vs **arterial/collector** road classes discussed; county may need to fund arterials as traffic grows; feeder road upgrades expected from developers.
- **Private roads:** **5-acre** minimum lot size referenced for development.
- Road maintenance: subdivision roads built to county highway specs; **developer maintains** initially, then **highway department** takes over after about **one year**.
- Process clarification: **two hearings** over two months but **one vote** at the end (per prior body action and planning attorney guidance).
- Motion (later withdrawn after assurance): provide commissioners **county road list** and **potable water map** before the next planning meeting; chair agreed to email maps **the next day** and post information on the **county website**; request for **14** printed road lists at a future public safety meeting.
- **Commissioner Barnhill** may propose changing **30-foot** right-of-way to **24-foot** next month.
- Distinction emphasized between **right-of-way** and **paved road width** (e.g., state highways may have wide pavement but different ROW).
