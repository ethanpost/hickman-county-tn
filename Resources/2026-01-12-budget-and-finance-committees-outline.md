#### 2. Property Tax Indexing Proposal
- New law allows counties to do indexing in year 2 of four-year reappraisal cycle
- State approved plan on previous Friday
- Purpose: stabilize tax base, avoid large increases, recoup revenue lost to ratio ($800,000)
- System: 300 neighborhoods identified (up from 9); properties adjusted based on sales within neighborhood
- Year 2 requires certified tax rate
- Prevents companies from calling for ratio in years 1 or 3
- Examples: Fair View Boulevard, General Forest, Riverview as separate neighborhoods
- If house sells for more/less than assessed value, entire neighborhood adjusts accordingly
- Motion to send to county commission this month (approved)
- Resolution needed for county commission approval

#### 3. EMS Cardiac Monitors and CPR Devices Financing
- Request for new Stryker Life Pack 35s and Lucas CPR devices
- Total cost: $555,625.04
- Stryker chosen over ZO due to included battery chargers and mounting devices
- Eight-year warranty included
- Financing options: 3-year lease at 0% interest or 6-year lease at 2% interest

- $30,000 grant available for first payment

- Opioid fund could purchase monitors but may be better for direct purchase rather than lease payments

- Allows time to explore other funding options
