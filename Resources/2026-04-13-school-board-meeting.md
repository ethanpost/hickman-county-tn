## Call to Order
- Meeting opened for the April 13 board meeting.
- Board observed a moment of silence and the Pledge of Allegiance.
- A new board member was welcomed at their first official meeting.

## Agenda and Minutes
- Board added the HCHS Apex Bank agreement to the agenda and approved the amended agenda.
- Board approved prior meeting minutes, including regular and special-called meetings from March.

## Special Recognition
- Employee of the Month recognition was presented to John West for service as bus driver and substitute teacher.
- Certificates were presented to student delegates who attended the TSBA/SCOPE conference.

## Consent Agenda
- Consent agenda items were approved by motion.

## Student Trips and Activities

### Tennessee Strong Youth Summit Request
- HCHS TNR Club requested approval for an overnight trip to Chattanooga (July 19-21).
- Trip purpose included tobacco/vaping prevention leadership training and student advocacy.
- Funding was reported as Tennessee Department of Health support (no school board funds).
- Board approved the trip request.

### East Hickman Cheer Camp Request
- East Hickman High School requested permission to attend UCA cheer camp at Vanderbilt (June 1-3).
- Board approved the travel request.

## Operational and Membership Items

### 2026-27 Attendance Zones
- Board reviewed and approved proposed attendance zones for the upcoming school year.

### TSBA Membership Dues
- Board discussed annual TSBA membership value (policy, legal, and legislative support).
- Roll call vote approved dues.

### Hickman County Chamber of Commerce Dues
- Board discussed increase in dues and asked for clearer return-on-investment details.
- Discussion included tourism, shop local efforts, sales tax impact, and business-education partnerships.
- Motion to continue membership passed, with one abstention noted due to conflict.

### Budget Committee Appointments
- Board appointed all board members to the budget committee.

## Action Items and Bids

### Intercom Maintenance and Repair Bid
- Board reviewed system-wide intercom maintenance needs after multiple issues and rising costs.
- One responsive bidder was received; contract intended to cover maintenance through next school year.
- Roll call vote approved the agreement.

### Automotive Class Training Equipment (HCHS)
- Board reviewed two bids for automotive trainers funded through an Innovative School Models grant.
- Lower bid (Georgia Education Solutions/ConsuLab) was selected and approved by roll call vote.
- Discussion confirmed equipment is industry-standard and supports hands-on troubleshooting skills.

### E-Rate Category 1 Broadband Bid
- Prior consortium agreement had expired, requiring local mini-bid process.
- Board reviewed three vendor responses; ENA was recommended and selected.
- District reported estimated annual savings of about $47,000 versus previous cost.
- Roll call vote approved the broadband contract.

### Internal Connections Licensing Bid
- Board reviewed renewal licensing for wireless access points and network switches.
- One response was received and approved by roll call vote.

### Managed Internet Broadband Services (Network Access Controller)
- Board reviewed multi-year licensing/service costs for network access control and security.
- Motion passed by roll call vote.

### Budget Amendments 30, 31, and 32
- Amendment 30: added insurance recovery funds and TCAT dual enrollment related funding.
- Amendment 31: included cleanup items and increased travel costs tied to new student information system training.
- Amendment 32: grant-related adjustments (including Perkins spending alignment near year-end).
- Board approved all three amendments by roll call vote.

### OPEB Discussion and Action
- Board reviewed OPEB fund status, eligibility, projected costs, and scenarios for expanding benefits to non-certified staff.
- Discussion covered longevity of funds, participation assumptions, and risk of earlier fund depletion under higher usage scenarios.
- Motion passed to table final OPEB action for a later meeting/work session.

### Policy Actions
- Board approved policy `1.8` (nepotism) on second reading.
- Board approved first reading of multiple TSBA-updated policies, including employee separation-related language revised with legal counsel input.
- Board approved additional policies listed as reviewed.

### HCHS Apex Bank Agreement
- Board discussed moving HCHS banking relationship to Apex Bank after multiple meetings and draft revisions.
- Discussion included sponsorship terms, expected support for athletics, and transition logistics between accounts.
- Motion passed to accept the agreement.

## Discussion Items

### Internal School Funds Audit
- Board reviewed annual internal school funds audit findings.
- Two findings were highlighted: sales tax handling on resale items/fundraisers and fundraiser authorization documentation at one school.
- Administration presented a corrective action plan and discussed additional training for schools/bookkeepers.

### FY Budget Development and Funding Outlook
- Administration requested additional work-session time to finalize budget development with consultant involvement.
- Preliminary funding notes included updated TISA estimate, property tax reappraisal estimate, and projected year-end fund balance impact.
- Discussion emphasized long-term structural funding pressure, salary mandate impacts, competitiveness, and avoiding one-year-only budget thinking.
- Board scheduled a special budget work session for April 29 at 6:30 PM.

### Transportation Seat Belt Safety
- Board discussed bus seat belt usage, enforcement challenges, driver distraction concerns, and student behavior.
- Transportation staff presented safety context, training requirements, inspection standards, and national statistics.
- Discussion noted buses are generally very safe, with particular risk during loading/unloading periods.

## Announcements
- Next regular board meeting: May 4 at Hickman County High School (recognition and awards).
- Legislative update included end-of-session topics such as vouchers, sports participation policy questions, and virtual school accountability.
- Additional board member updates included local school event notes.

## Adjournment
- Board approved motion to adjourn.
