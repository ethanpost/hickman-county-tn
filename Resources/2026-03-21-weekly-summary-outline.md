### School Board / Budget

- School board meetings are **available online**; transcripts can be reviewed  
- Current budget cycle is in progress  
- Reported **deficit of at least ~$1 million** (final number expected in coming months)

**Teacher Pay**

- State requirement to bring **starting teacher pay to ~$50,000**  
- Current starting pay appears to be ~~**4–5% below that level (~~$5,000 difference)**  
- Adjustments at the low end expected to **impact salaries across all levels**

**Budget Impact**

- Combined salary and benefit adjustments could result in **~$1–2 million increase (*my opinion)***

### School Funding Structure

- Funding based on a **per-student formula**
  - Base amount per student  
  - Additional funding for:
    - Disabilities  
    - Non-English speakers  
    - Dyslexia and other needs
- Approximate funding split:
  - **~70% state**
  - **~30% county**
- Additional funding sources contribute beyond that formula (e.g., meal programs)

### School System Observations

- No discussion observed regarding:  **(*my opinion)***
  - **Reducing number of teachers**
  - **Changing student-to-teacher ratios**
- Student population:
  - Declining over the past ~5 years  
  - Current estimate: **~2,800–2,900 students**
- Estimated ratios:
  - **~1 teacher per 18 students**   **(*my opinion)***
  - Administrative ratios not yet determined

### Mental Health Services

- Discussion of bringing in **Mercy TN**
  - Organization with locations in **Franklin and possibly Nashville**  
  - Would provide **in-school mental health counseling**
- Model:
  - Services placed within schools  
  - Provider bills **insurance directly**
- Current usage:
  - ~400 students receiving services  
  - Primarily **older students (approx. age 10+)  (*my opinion)***
- Proposal includes:
  - Expanding to **full-time counselors in schools**  
  - Hiring within the county

### EMS (Emergency Services)

- EMS currently **short on paramedics**  
- Was reportedly **fully staffed as recently as September**  
- Staff departures linked to:
  - **Higher pay elsewhere**
  - **Working conditions**

---

### Budget Trends / General Observations

- County budgets have **increased consistently over time**  
- Increases tied to:
  - Salaries  
  - Benefits  
  - Staffing
- Hiring patterns:
  - New roles added as responsibilities change  
  - Once positions are filled, they are **rarely reduced  (*my opinion)***

### County Commission (Upcoming Meeting)

- Agenda item: allowing Sheriff’s Department to **enforce Tennessee nuisance laws**

**Scope of Laws**

- Door-to-door sales / traveling contractors  
- Solicitation activity (including unverifiable causes)  
- Broader categories (including prostitution-related activity)

**Context**

- Seasonal issues with:
  - Traveling sales crews (e.g., driveway work)  
  - Individuals soliciting money for unclear or unrelated causes
- Laws allow:
  - Removal or fining of individuals  
  - Potential **confiscation of assets**
