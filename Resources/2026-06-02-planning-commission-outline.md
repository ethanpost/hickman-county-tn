### Proposed Amendment — A1 Private Airstrips (Special Exception)
- County currently lacks zoning allowance for private airstrips; existing airport rules apply to Centerville Municipal Airport with stricter requirements than a grass strip.
- Amendment modeled after Blount County’s resolution; applicants must complete FAA requirements before county special exception.
- Public notice: applicant must notify abutting owners and all owners within 2,000 ft by registered mail (applicant expense, not county); registered mail 10 days prior; standard sign posting by building official.
- Letter of “agreement” with existing airstrips within 5-mile radius discussed; may be changed to “acknowledgement.”
- Landing strip limited to small single-engine fixed-wing craft only (Section B 3.170B).
- Concerns raised: sole arbiter role of building commissioner vs. former committee process; neighbor impacts (noise, night flying); 30-day appeal window; FAA standards trump local development standards; option to make use permitted by right in A1 (site plan to planning commission only, not county commission) vs. rezoning route to county board.
- Anecdote: helicopter buzzing at ~125 ft altitude; speaker cited 37 years of flight experience (32 in helicopters).
- Motion to table until next month for further consideration; second received; roll call vote: nine yes to table.
- Tabled. Chairman urged members to consider carefully before proceeding (“Pandora’s box” concern).

### Site Plan Approval — Southern Consulting / Our Market (Hwy 46 and 100)
- Trent Smith (Southern Consulting) and Bruce (construction) presented site plan for diesel truck refueling awning at Our Market on the corner of Highways 46 and 100.
- Four fuel islands serving four vehicles at one time; new canopy; concrete pad using existing inlets/outlets.
- Intended primarily for local traffic: log trucks, dump trucks; semis possible but trucks planned to route around back and exit on Highway 100 (not 46).
- Sewer/water: should not increase sewage needs vs. original gas station proposal.
- Safety concerns: busy intersection; westbound-only exit from gas station onto 100; ingress/egress largely a DOT matter, not planning commission purview.
- Condition suggested: signage routing truck traffic to Highway 100.
- Discussion of removed median/island on 100 affecting eastbound turns into the station; existing large vehicles (e.g., 24-foot feather-light cattle trailer) block current islands—new area intended to alleviate congestion.
- Roll call vote: all yes.
- Site plan approved.
