- Resolution 2614 was discussed as a possible change to the wording for rezoning requests.
- Concern was raised that the language may be too broad if a property owner is denied one rezoning request, such as A1 to R2, and later wants to apply for a different category, such as R1.
- The purpose of the waiting period was described as a cooling-off period to prevent denied rezoning requests from being immediately refiled when nothing about the property or circumstances had changed.

- The body discussed making the language clearer, potentially with help from the county attorney, so a denied request would not improperly block a different rezoning request.
