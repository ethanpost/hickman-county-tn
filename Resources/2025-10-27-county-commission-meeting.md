## Unfinished Business — Resolutions

### Resolution 2529 — R1 Suburban Residential District Amendments
- Sponsored by Commissioner Calakott and Commissioner Clark
- **First amendment:** Removed words “to be subdivided” from section one part one — approved by voice vote
- **Purpose (as explained):** For A1 to R1 rezoning in county growth area, require 30-foot right-of-way road suitable for two-lane traffic and potable water at property; property owners may upgrade road/water to meet standards; aimed at managing growth and infrastructure before higher density
- Extensive discussion: applies to growth-area R1 (smaller than 1-acre lots, e.g., 7/10 acre vs. 1 acre on A1); does not change land use plan or county-wide agricultural zoning by itself; Williamson County planning lessons cited (Hidden Valley Lakes example)
- **Motion to remove potable water requirement:** Failed roll call 3 yes, 11 no
- **Motion to change 30-foot to 25-foot right-of-way:** Failed roll call 3 yes, 11 no; discussion noted ~81 miles of 20–25 ft right-of-way roads (34 miles A/B), 26 ft pavement industry standard, road list in 5-foot increments
- **Final adoption:** Resolution 2529 adopted (vote recorded as 8 yes, 5 no, 1 pass; after vote change, 9 yes, 5 no per chair)
