## School Budget (Fund 141)

### School Board Presentation
- School board met after recent county commission meeting; board decided to accept a **4-cent property tax** increase for the current budget.
- Maintenance project list unchanged from prior request—**no additions**.
- School representative thanked the committee for considering the 4-cent request.

### Procedure and Rules
- Committee can only **approve or reject** the school budget as a whole, not line-by-line.
- Motion to set aside rules so the **budget committee chairman** could participate more freely in debate (still cannot make motions); passed by voice vote.

### Discussion — Maintenance of Effort and Revenue
- Chairman noted **maintenance of effort (MOE)** increase of about **11%** vs. **4% cap** communicated to county general departments.
- County growth discussed at about **3%**; appraisal values up but rates coming down.
- Prior-year MOE about **$6.1 million**; this request adds about **$710,000** (~**11.5%**).
- **TISA** funding described as up **$286,000** on a **$25 million** base (~**1%**), while the local request is **$710,000**.
- **Local option sales tax** discussed as a possible lever to reduce or eliminate the **4-cent** property tax ask.
- Commissioner Murray noted school system gave up **6 cents** last year (~**4 cents** equivalent now) with understanding it might be requested back.
- Sales tax line on budget described as adjusting the **MOE floor** (e.g., budget **3.3** must be met)—school gets **50%** of actual collections, not a flat **$325,000** add-on.
- **Capital projects** (~**$400,000+**) discussed as separate from MOE; funding outside MOE would be county obligation.

### Vote on School Budget as Submitted
- Motion to **approve as resubmitted** the current school budget (4-cent increase).
- Roll call: **4 yes, 9 no, 1 absent**; motion **fails**—school budget **not approved**.
- Follow-up meetings noted: regular budget meeting **June 8**; **May 14** special meeting **cancelled** unless eight members could attend; **May 28** also on revised schedule.

### Capital Projects — Deferred Then Addressed via Guidance
- Before recess, commissioners debated whether to discuss **~$405,000** capital request separately; concern about deciding without clarity on property tax and sales tax pieces.
- **10-minute recess** called (return ~**6:15**).

### Post-Recess Guidance to School Board
- Informal consensus discussed: **2-cent** property tax instead of **4 cents**, and pair back **local option sales tax** so MOE increase is about **6%**.
- **$405,000** capital treated as **one-time** from **Fund 151** fund balance—**no new tax** for that piece.
- **$45,000** capital expenditure could come from fund balance without a dedicated revenue stream.
- Motion to send **Fund 141** budget back to school board with those tax numbers and the capital understanding (direction only, not final approval).
- Roll call: **11 yes, 2 no, 1 absent**; motion carries.
- Chair stated this guidance covers both operating budget and capital framing for school’s resubmission.
