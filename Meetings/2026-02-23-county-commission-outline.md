## Public Comment Period
- **Resolution 2607 (Taylor Creek Campground Rezoning)**
  - Multiple community members spoke in opposition to proposed campground rezoning
  - Concerns raised:
    - Property located in residential community and flood plain
    - Inadequate road infrastructure (narrow, one-lane, gravel roads with blind spots)
    - No public access to recreation from property
    - Concerns about sewage disposal and environmental impact on Duck River
    - Weekend traffic and recreational vehicle issues already burdening area
    - Questions about spot zoning policy and property owner's experience/capital
    - Concern that commercial rezoning could allow other uses beyond campground
  - Note: No vote on this resolution tonight; will be on next month's agenda
