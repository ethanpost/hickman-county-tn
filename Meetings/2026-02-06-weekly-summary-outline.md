# Weekly Summary: Health Safety and Properties Committee Meeting  
**Date:** February 2, 2026

---

## Main Focus

- **Winter Storm Debrief:**  
  The committee dedicated most of the meeting to reviewing the recent winter storm response. The county’s efforts were **praised as a model** for others in the state.

---

## Key Infrastructure Needs Identified

- **Generators for EMS Stations:**  
  - Cost estimate: **~$40,000–$60,000 per unit**  
  - **3 stations** currently mentioned as priority
- **Road/Access Improvements:**  
  - Improved access to **emergency radio towers** is needed
- **Larger Fuel Tanks:**  
  - **911/Nine Mile Ridge site** requires an upgrade  

---

## Context & Additional Notes

- The committee noted the **response was strong** despite significant destruction.
- The 2026 storm was described as **worse** than the notable 1994 ice storm (colder, more widespread).
- Effective **response coordination** was attributed to ongoing EMA planning and scenario training.
- **Generators are especially needed**:  
  - Power loss at prevented use of fuel pumps, requiring fuel suppliers to deliver directly to vehicles with trucks.

## 🏛️ Planning Commission Meeting Summary  
**Date:** February 3, 2026

---

### **Main Topic**

- **State Law Change: Special Exceptions**
  - Authority over special exceptions would shift from the **Board of Zoning Appeals (BZA)** to the **Zoning Administrator** (appointed by the mayor).
  - The BZA would act solely as an appellate board.
  - Action was **tabled** to address procedural details: neighbor notification, sign posting, and public hearing process.
  - **Key Impact:** Centralizes decision-making with a mayoral appointee; details still under discussion.

---

### **Lesson Learned**

> **Zoning change requests often fail due to poor preparation.**  
>  
> **Tip:**  
> - Review the relevant zoning laws.
> - Prepare detailed plans, documentation, and supporting images.
> - Clearly explain the reasons for your request.  
>  
> *Taking these steps improves your chances of success.*
