## Key Issues and Recommendations

### Tennessee Waste to Jobs Act
- **Position**: Opposition recommended
- **Concerns**:
  - Creates Producer Responsibility Organizations (PROs) with mandatory membership
  - Forces redistribution of collected funds through bureaucratic structure
  - While voluntary for small counties initially, could eventually become mandatory
  - Costs would be passed to consumers through product prices
- **Action Needed**: Contact county and surrounding counties to coordinate opposition if legislation advances
